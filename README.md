## About

Zenty Finance is an AI-powered personal finance assistant that helps you understand, track, and improve your financial health — all through a natural language chatbot interface. Built with Python, machine learning, and NLP on the backend, and a modern TypeScript/React frontend, Zenty makes personal finance feel less overwhelming and more intuitive.

---

## Features

- **AI Financial Chatbot** — natural language conversations about your finances powered by ML & NLP
- **Transaction Tracking** — log and categorize income and expenses automatically
- **Budget Management** — set budgets and get alerts when you're close to limits
- **Savings Goals** — track progress toward financial milestones
- **Stock Portfolio Tracking** — monitor your investments in real time
- **Analytics & Insights** — visual breakdowns of spending patterns and financial health

## The AI Chatbot

The heart of Zenty is its conversational AI assistant, built using natural language processing and machine learning models trained on financial data. You can ask it things like:

> *"How much did I spend on food last month?"*
> *"Am I on track to hit my savings goal?"*
> *"What's my biggest spending category this week?"*

The chatbot interprets intent, queries your financial data, and responds with personalized insights — no finance degree required.
